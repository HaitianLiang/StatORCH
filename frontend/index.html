External acquisition

Regime-dependent, not universal dominance

REALDISP and Gas Sensor Array Drift use fixed subject/time protocols with charged target pilots.

REALDISP

StatOrch leads the evaluated performance–cost AUC and is strong at moderate displacement; under severe induced displacement, BestFixed can yield lower prevalence error.

Gas Drift

GSM-AFA is strongest overall; StatOrch is second. This is evidence for specialized orchestration rather than a new universally superior AFA algorithm.

Statistical scope

Severe REALDISP has three eligible held-out subjects and Gas Drift four target batches, so these are fixed-protocol external results, not unrestricted population guarantees.
